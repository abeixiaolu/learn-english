# 02 — 一般现在时

## 要点

### 1. 基本结构

**主语 + 动词原形**（第三人称单数加 -s/-es）

- I **work** from home.
- She **works** from home.
- We **have** a standup every Monday.

### 2. 使用场景

- **习惯 / 日常**：I drink coffee every morning.
- **事实 / 规律**：The standup **starts** at 9 a.m.
- **固定安排**：The sprint **ends** on Friday.

### 3. 第三人称单数（he / she / it / 单数名词）

| 规则                            | 例子                                 |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 一般加 -s                       | work → work**s**，check → check**s** |
| 以 s/x/ch/sh/o 结尾加 -es       | watch → watch**es**，go → go**es**   |
| 辅音 + y 结尾，变 y 为 i 加 -es | study → stud**ies**                  |

### 4. 频率副词的位置

| 类型     | 位置                            | 例子                                        |
| -------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 实义动词 | **副词在主语后、动词前**        | I **usually** work from home.               |
| be 动词  | **副词在 be 后**                | She **is** usually online in the afternoon. |
| 否定句   | 副词在 don't/doesn't 和动词之间 | He **doesn't** often work overtime.         |

常见频率副词（从高到低）：always → usually → often → sometimes → rarely → never

### 5. 否定与疑问

- 否定：don't / doesn't + **动词原形**
  - I **don't** work on weekends.
  - She **doesn't** reply at night.
- 疑问：**Do / Does** + 主语 + **动词原形**
  - **Do** you often have meetings?
  - **Does** he check email every morning?

### 6. 常见中式错误

| 中文思维       | 错误                         | 正确                                |
| -------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| 他工作         | He work                      | He **works**                        |
| 我经常忙       | I often am busy              | I **am often** busy                 |
| 她不做加班     | She doesn't works overtime   | She **doesn't work** overtime       |
| 你每天开会吗？ | You have meetings every day? | **Do** you have meetings every day? |
