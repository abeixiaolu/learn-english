# 07 — 现在进行时 vs 一般现在时

## 要点

### 1. 基本形式

**现在进行时**表示说话时正在发生、或当前一段时间正在进行的动作。结构是：

**主语 + am / is / are + 动词 -ing**

- I am fixing a bug right now.
- She is reviewing the PR at the moment.
- They are deploying the service now.

### 2. 现在进行时 vs 一般现在时

| 时态 | 用途 | 时间信号 | 例子 |
|------|------|----------|------|
| 现在进行时 | 正在做、当前阶段在做 | now, right now, at the moment, currently, today | I am writing a script right now. |
| 一般现在时 | 习惯、日常、事实 | every day, usually, always, often | I write scripts every week. |

同一动词，时间信号不同，时态就不同：

- She reviews code every morning.（习惯）
- She is reviewing code right now.（此刻正在做）

### 3. 肯定、否定、疑问

| 类型 | 结构 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|
| 肯定 | 主语 + am/is/are + V-ing | He is debugging the API. |
| 否定 | 主语 + am/is/are + not + V-ing | I am not working overtime today. |
| 疑问 | Am/Is/Are + 主语 + V-ing？ | Are you waiting for the build? |

简短回答：

- Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
- Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.

### 4. -ing 拼写提醒

| 规则 | 原形 | -ing |
|------|------|------|
| 一般直接加 -ing | check, deploy | checking, deploying |
| 以不发音 e 结尾，去 e 再加 -ing | write, use | writing, using |
| 重读闭音节，双写末尾辅音再加 -ing | run, get | running, getting |

### 5. 状态动词通常不用进行时

表示状态、感觉、看法、拥有的动词，一般用**一般现在时**，不用进行时：

know, want, need, like, love, hate, believe, understand, remember, prefer, seem, own, have（表拥有）

- I need your help. ✓
- I am needing your help. ✗
- She understands the issue. ✓
- She is understanding the issue. ✗

注意：`have` 表「拥有」时不用进行时；表动作时可以：

- I have a laptop.（拥有）
- I am having a meeting now.（正在开会）

### 6. 常见中式错误

| 中文思维 | 错误 | 正确 |
|----------|------|------|
| 我正在知道 | I am knowing the answer. | I **know** the answer. |
| 她现在需要帮助 | She is needing help now. | She **needs** help now. |
| 我每天都在检查日志 | I am checking the logs every day. | I **check** the logs every day. |
| 他现在修 bug | He fix a bug now. | He **is fixing** a bug now. |
