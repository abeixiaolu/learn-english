# 01 — be 动词 + 主系表

## 要点

### 1. be 动词的形式

| 人称 | 肯定 | 否定 | 疑问 |
|------|------|------|------|
| I | am / I'm | am not / I'm not | Am I …? |
| you | are / you're | are not / aren't | Are you …? |
| he/she/it | is / he's | is not / isn't | Is he …? |
| we/they | are / they're | are not / aren't | Are they …? |

### 2. 主系表结构

**主语 + be + 表语**（名词 / 形容词 / 介词短语）

- I **am** an engineer.
- She **is** from Shanghai.
- They **are** in a meeting.

表语是形容词时，**英文通常不加「很」**：

- ❌ I am very busy.（口语里 very 可以，但别从中文「我很忙」硬套）
- ✅ I **am** busy. / I **am** quite busy.

### 3. 常见中式错误

| 中文思维 | 错误 | 正确 |
|----------|------|------|
| 我是工程师 | I am engineer | I **am an** engineer |
| 我是忙 | I am very busy（硬套「很」） | I **am** busy |
| 他在家吗？ | He at home? | **Is** he at home? |
| 我们不是同事 | We not colleagues | We **are not** colleagues |

### 4. 自我介绍常用句型

- My name **is** … / I **am** …
- I **am** a … (job)
- I **am** from …
- Nice to meet you. I **am** …
