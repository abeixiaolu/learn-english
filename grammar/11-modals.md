# 11 — should / must / have to

## 要点

### 1. should：建议、劝告

`should + 动词原形` 表示“应该”，语气是建议，不是强制命令。

- You should check the schedule.
- We should update the document.
- She shouldn't deploy without a review.

`should` 不随人称变化，后面直接接动词原形。

### 2. must：语气最强的“必须”

`must + 动词原形` 常表示说话人强调的规则、强烈必要性或禁令。

- You must ask for approval.
- We must finish this before the deadline.
- You mustn't share the password.

`must` 语气通常比 `should`、`have to` 更强。

### 3. have to：外部规定带来的“必须”

`have to / has to + 动词原形` 常表示公司规定、流程、客观安排带来的义务。

- I have to submit the ticket today.
- She has to prepare the report.
- They have to follow the process.

第三人称单数用 `has to`，其他人称用 `have to`。

### 4. should vs must vs have to

| 表达 | 语气 | 常见场景 | 例子 |
|------|------|----------|------|
| should | 建议 | 劝告、更好做法 | You should double-check the data. |
| must | 很强 | 规则、强烈必要、禁令 | You must ask for approval. |
| have to | 义务 | 外部安排、流程要求 | I have to join the call at 3. |

如果只是“最好这样做”，优先 `should`；如果是硬性规则或强烈必要，用 `must` 或 `have to`。

### 5. 否定含义不同

| 结构 | 含义 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|
| shouldn't | 不该做（劝告） | You shouldn't stay late every day. |
| mustn't | 禁止做 | You mustn't share production access. |
| don't / doesn't have to | 不必做（没有义务） | You don't have to work overtime tonight. |

注意：`don't have to` ≠ `mustn't`。

- You don't have to stay late. → 你可以不加班。
- You mustn't stay late. → 你不准加班。

### 6. 疑问句

| 类型 | 结构 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|
| should | Should + 主语 + 动词原形？ | Should I choose this option? |
| must | Must + 主语 + 动词原形？ | Must we upgrade it today? |
| have to | Do/Does + 主语 + have to + 动词原形？ | Do I have to submit it now? |

### 7. 形式提醒

- `should / must / have to` 后面都接**动词原形**。
- 不要写 `should to check`、`must to finish`。
- `he / she / it` 用 `has to`，不是 `have to`。
- 先分清：建议（should）、强制（must）、外部义务（have to）、不必（don't have to）。
