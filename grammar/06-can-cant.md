# 06 — can / can't

## 要点

### 1. 基本用途

**can / can't** 表示能力、可能性或礼貌请求。后面接**动词原形**。

- I can write SQL queries.
- She can't join the meeting today.
- Can you review this PR?

### 2. 肯定、否定、疑问

| 类型 | 结构 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|
| 肯定 | 主语 + can + 动词原形 | I can fix this bug. |
| 否定 | 主语 + can't / cannot + 动词原形 | He can't access the staging server. |
| 疑问 | Can + 主语 + 动词原形？ | Can you help me with this ticket? |

回答：

- Yes, I can.
- No, I can't.

### 3. can 不变人称

**can** 不随主语变化，没有 cans。

- He can deploy the service.
- She can write unit tests.
- They can work from home.

不要写：

- He cans deploy the service.

### 4. 能力 vs 请求

| 用途 | 例子 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| 能力 | I can speak English. | 会不会、能不能做到 |
| 请求 | Can you send me the logs? | 请对方做事，语气直接礼貌 |
| 许可 | Can I leave early today? | 征求允许 |

职场里请求帮助，用 **Can you…?** 比中文直译「你能不能……」更自然、更简洁。

### 5. can't = cannot

口语和日常写作多用 **can't**；正式场合可用 **cannot**。

- I can't open the file.
- We cannot access the production database without approval.

### 6. 常见中式错误

| 中文思维 | 错误 | 正确 |
|----------|------|------|
| 他会部署 | He cans deploy. | He **can** deploy. |
| 我不能加入 | I can't joining. | I **can't join**. |
| 你能不能帮我？ | You can help me? | **Can you** help me? |
| 我可不可以看一下？ | I can look at it? | **Can I** look at it? |
