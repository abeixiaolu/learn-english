# 05 — Past Simple

## 要点

### 1. 基本用途

一般过去时表示**过去某个明确时间发生的动作或状态**。

- I fixed a bug yesterday.
- She joined the meeting at 3 p.m.
- We deployed the service last night.

常见时间标记：

| 时间词 / 短语 | 含义 |
|---------------|------|
| yesterday | 昨天 |
| last night | 昨晚 |
| last week | 上周 |
| two days ago | 两天前 |
| this morning | 今天早上（如果现在已过早上） |
| in 2025 | 在 2025 年 |

### 2. 动词过去式

规则动词通常加 **-ed**：

| 原形 | 过去式 | 例子 |
|------|--------|------|
| check | checked | I checked the logs yesterday. |
| review | reviewed | She reviewed the PR last night. |
| deploy | deployed | We deployed the service on Friday. |
| open | opened | He opened a ticket this morning. |

不规则动词要单独记：

| 原形 | 过去式 | 例子 |
|------|--------|------|
| go | went | I went to the office yesterday. |
| find | found | We found a bug in the login flow. |
| write | wrote | She wrote a short report. |
| have | had | They had a meeting after lunch. |
| make | made | He made a small change. |

### 3. 否定句：didn't + 动词原形

一般过去时的否定用 **didn't + 动词原形**，后面的动词不要再变过去式。

- I didn't check the logs.
- She didn't join the meeting.
- We didn't deploy the service.

不要写：

- I didn't checked the logs.
- She didn't joined the meeting.

### 4. 疑问句：Did + 主语 + 动词原形

一般过去时的普通疑问句用 **Did** 开头，动词也用原形。

- Did you fix the bug?
- Did he open a ticket?
- Did they review the document?

回答时可以用：

- Yes, I did.
- No, I didn't.

### 5. be 动词的过去式

**am / is → was**，**are → were**。

| 现在 | 过去 |
|------|------|
| I am busy. | I was busy yesterday. |
| She is at the office. | She was at the office this morning. |
| They are in the meeting room. | They were in the meeting room. |

否定：

- I wasn't busy yesterday.
- They weren't in the meeting room.

疑问：

- Was she at the office?
- Were they in the meeting room?

### 6. already / yesterday 的纠偏

中文里常说「已经」「昨天」，英文要根据时间选择时态：

| 中文意思 | 今天先练的表达 |
|----------|----------------|
| 我昨天已经修了这个 bug。 | I fixed the bug yesterday. |
| 她今天早上已经发了报告。 | She sent the report this morning. |
| 我们上周已经讨论过这个问题。 | We discussed this issue last week. |

今天重点是**明确过去时间 + 一般过去时**。不要因为中文有「已经」就忽略过去时。
